Strategy Rules

0.786 Fibonacci Continuation

Trade in the direction of the trend. Follow the pullback. Enter with precision.

1. Market condition

  • Market must be in a clear uptrend or downtrend
  • Use 4H or 1H for analysis
  • Avoid sideways / choppy markets

2. Draw Fibonacci

  • From the last swing low (0) to the last swing high (1) — or the reverse in a downtrend
  • Focus on the 0.786 retracement level

3. Entry Type 1 — Direct

  • Price pulls back to 0.786
  • Enter directly in the 0.786 area
  • No lower-timeframe confirmation needed

4. Entry Type 2 — Confirmation (LTF)

  • Price pulls back to 0.786
  • Switch to 5M / 1M
  • Wait for a Change of Character (CHoCH)
  • Find an FVG or OB at the CHoCH area
  • Enter from the FVG / OB

5. Stop loss

  • Type 1: below the swing low / fib level 1 (small buffer)
  • Type 2: below the OB or CHoCH swing low (small buffer)

6. Take profit

  • Last swing high (fib level 0)
  • Minimum RR 1:3 or higher

7. Risk management

  • Minimum RR must be 1:3 — only take trades that meet or exceed it
  • Never risk more than 1–2% of capital on a single trade
